Bad credit business loan questions

Can I really apply with bad credit?

Yes. There is no minimum FICO score required to apply, and bad credit is not automatically a block. Most top financing options have a minimum of just 660 FICO — below that, your application can still be reviewed and other options may be considered. Funding is subject to review and never guaranteed.

What credit score do I need?

You need no minimum score just to apply. For the top financing options, most have a minimum of just 660 FICO. If you are close to or above 660, with $10,000+ in monthly gross sales and 4+ months in business, you match the headline profile for the top working capital options.

I make less than $10,000 a month. Am I wasting my time?

Not necessarily. $10,000+ in monthly gross sales is a headline criterion for the top working capital options — it is not the criterion for every option. Equipment financing and startup options have different criteria, so you can still apply to see what may match. No option is promised.

I've been in business less than 4 months. Can I still apply?

Yes. Four-plus months is the headline bar for the top working capital options. Startup options have different criteria, so a newer business can still start an application and be reviewed. Funding is subject to review and never guaranteed.

How fast could I get funded?

The application starts in about 15 seconds. Same-day funding is possible for qualified businesses, after review. Timing is never guaranteed.

Is approval guaranteed if I meet the numbers above?

No. Meeting the headline criteria — 660 FICO for most top options, $10,000+ in monthly gross sales, 4+ months in business — describes the strongest profile, but every application is reviewed individually and funding is never guaranteed.
